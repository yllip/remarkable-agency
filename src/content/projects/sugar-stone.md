For eleven years Sugar Stone made confectionery for other brands. The recipes
were theirs, the reputation was not, and the margin belonged to whoever's logo
was on the box.

## A brand built to be picked up

Going direct meant the packaging had to do the work of a shopfront. The system
is built around a single, unapologetically sweet palette and a wordmark with
enough weight to hold its own against the pastels, so a box reads from across an
aisle and still rewards being held.

## Restraint where it counts

Everything else stays quiet. One typeface, one grid, product photography that
shows the thing at actual size. The flourish is spent entirely on colour, which
keeps a growing range coherent as new flavours land.

The first own-label run sold through in nine days, and two of the original
contract clients now stock the brand alongside their own.
